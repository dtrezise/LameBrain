## Gate 0 — Human owner required before first paid customer

- [ ] **Founder:** choose formation jurisdiction and entity type with counsel/CPA; file entity and obtain EIN.
  - Evidence: filed formation record, EIN letter, registered agent, ownership record.
- [ ] **Founder + trademark counsel:** run clearance for “Lame Brain Ideas,” tagline, and identity; decide keep/rename.
  - Evidence: written search opinion, approved name, filing decision, moderated audience-test notes.
- [ ] **Founder + counsel:** approve customer MSA/SOW, partner agreement, NDA option, media release, contractor/IP assignment, website terms, and refund policy.
  - Evidence: dated approved templates and signing workflow.
- [ ] **Founder + insurance broker:** bind required coverage.
  - Evidence: certificates and limits for general, professional, cyber, equipment, event, and workers-compensation coverage as applicable.
- [ ] **Finance owner:** open business bank, bookkeeping, expense approval, tax reserve, invoicing, and payment/refund accounts.
  - Evidence: reconciled opening balances, chart of accounts, test invoice/payment/refund.
- [ ] **Operations owner:** provision `hello@lamebrainideas.com` or update every public contact link to an active controlled address.
  - Evidence: inbound/outbound/delivery test and two-person recovery access.
- [ ] **Trust owner:** approve the actual tool list, data flow, retention schedule, deletion procedure, access roster, backup, and incident contacts.
  - Evidence: completed tool register and tabletop incident exercise.
- [ ] **Accessibility owner:** test public site and intake path with keyboard, screen reader, zoom, contrast, and reduced motion; define accommodation workflow.
  - Evidence: dated report, resolved critical findings, public contact route.
- [ ] **Delivery owner:** run two simulated cases and one counsel-reviewed intake test.
  - Evidence: completed artifacts, time logs, QA results, referral decisions.
- [ ] **Partnerships owner:** sign three credible pilot hosts.
  - Evidence: dates, audience, recruitment responsibility, venue/remote setup, consent, and success measure.
- [ ] **Founder:** approve first $35,000 tranche and identify committed cash source.
  - Evidence: signed authorization, bank evidence, spend controls.

## Gate 1 — Release paid-pilot tranche

- [ ] 30 qualified discovery participants identified across target channels.
- [ ] Three host recruitment plans are active.
- [ ] Intake, scheduling, payment, scope acceptance, cancellation, refund, and data-deletion paths pass end-to-end tests.
- [ ] Public email and response SLA are operational.
- [ ] First two media pieces have source notes, transcripts, and consent records.
- [ ] No unresolved critical legal, trademark, insurance, security, privacy, or accessibility blocker.

## Gate 2 — Release completion buffer

- [ ] Six paid cases completed.
- [ ] At least two offers have paid evidence.
- [ ] Price, hours, direct cost, rework, refund, outcome, and acquisition source are recorded for every case.
- [ ] Customer experience and contribution are acceptable or a redesign is documented.
- [ ] No material trust incident remains unresolved.

## Day-90 decision packet

- [ ] 12 paid-case target result, including misses and cancellations.
- [ ] Funnel by channel and host.
- [ ] Economics by offer and customer segment.
- [ ] Outcome and follow-on evidence.
- [ ] Brand-test and referral evidence.
- [ ] Eight-episode media result and qualified actions.
- [ ] Updated 24-month base and downside cash case.
- [ ] Written repeat, narrow, redesign, pause, or stop recommendation.

## Explicitly not authorized

- A permanent lease, build-out, or retail staffing plan
- Rewards-crowdfunding platform ownership
- Securities crowdfunding, investor matching, recommendations, or transaction compensation
- Equity interests as customer compensation
- Franchising or licensing the operating model
- National paid-media scaling before local channel proof
