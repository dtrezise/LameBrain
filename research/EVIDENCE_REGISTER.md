# Lame Brain Ideas — Evidence register

**Last reviewed:** 2026-10-07
**Scope:** company-launch claims, assumptions, targets, and boundaries

## Claim classes

- **Sourced:** supported by a named external source.
- **Calculated:** derived from cited inputs or the financial model.
- **Management assumption:** editable commercial input with no primary proof yet.
- **Pilot target:** desired result used as a decision gate, not a forecast.
- **Policy:** deliberate company boundary.
- **Unknown:** requires evidence before a decision.
