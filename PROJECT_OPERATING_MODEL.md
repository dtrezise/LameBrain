# Lame Brain Ideas - Project Operating Model

- **Working status:** Feasibility and prospectus development
- **Decision posture:** Evidence before expansion
- **Primary recommendation:** Validate a lean studio before committing to a flagship store, owned funding platform, or franchise system

## Assigned agentic roles

Codex is operating as an integrated project team with the following roles. These are analytical and production roles; they do not replace licensed legal, accounting, securities, real-estate, or patent professionals.

| Role | Mandate | Primary output |
|---|---|---|
| Managing Partner / Investment Committee Lead | Reconcile the concept with investability, sequence work, and issue the feasibility verdict | Executive recommendation and stage gates |
| Venture Architect | Convert the concept into a coherent business model, offer ladder, and rollout thesis | Business architecture and roadmap |
| Market Research Lead | Establish demand signals, substitutes, competitive forces, and research gaps | Market study and evidence register |
| Retail Experience & Operations Lead | Design the store footprint, customer journey, staffing, capacity, and SOP requirements | Pilot operating model |
| Financial Modeling & Feasibility Lead | Build auditable unit economics, five-year projections, scenarios, cash needs, and rollout economics | Formula-driven Excel model |
| Product & AI Systems Architect | Define the workflow platform, data boundaries, human review, and defensible operating knowledge | AI/data/IP architecture |
| Risk, IP & Regulatory Workstream Lead | Identify legal and regulatory dependencies and specify where counsel is mandatory | Risk register and compliance gates |
| Brand & Customer Experience Strategist | Test the category language, promise, name, tagline, and service packaging | Positioning and validation plan |
| Evidence & Deliverable Steward | Separate source facts, external evidence, model assumptions, and recommendations | Source analysis, citations, and QA |

## Applied skills

- Public-share source recovery and provenance analysis
- Current-source market and regulatory research
- Venture feasibility and business-model design
- Retail service operations and capacity planning
- Financial modeling, scenario analysis, and cash planning
- Professional prospectus authoring and visual verification
- AI workflow, confidentiality, and data-governance design
- Legal issue spotting and referral-boundary design
- Repository setup, documentation, and handoff QA

## Decision rules

1. Source statements are treated as concept inputs, not facts.
2. External claims require a dated, reviewable source.
3. Model inputs are visibly editable and distinguished from formulas.
4. Crowdfunding, investor, equity, and franchise economics are not credited before their legal and operational gates are met.
5. The flagship lease is not signed until paid demand is demonstrated in a lower-capital pilot.
6. Customer idea data is not entered into production AI tools until retention, training, access, and deletion terms are approved.
7. No output is described as legal advice, patent clearance, a valuation opinion, or an investment recommendation.

## Phase ownership

| Phase | Lead roles | Exit decision |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Discovery | Market Research, Brand, Venture Architecture | Is the problem urgent and the promise understandable? |
| 1. Paid pop-up | Retail Ops, Financial Modeling, Evidence | Can paid sessions acquire customers and reach contribution targets? |
| 2. Flagship studio | Retail Ops, AI Systems, Venture Architecture | Can one location reach repeatable break-even economics? |
| 3. Digital membership | AI Systems, Operations, Brand | Can retention and remote delivery improve LTV without eroding trust? |
| 4. Funding partnerships | Risk/Regulatory, Venture Architecture | Can rewards or securities funding be delivered legally through qualified partners? |
| 5. Multi-unit rollout | Investment Committee, Finance, Operations | Are unit economics, training, quality, and governance replicable? |

## Required human specialists

- Securities counsel and broker-dealer/funding-portal advisor before any investor matching, transaction-based compensation, or securities offering
- IP counsel/patent attorney or agent for patentability, freedom-to-operate, and filing work
- Trademark counsel for full clearance of the name, tagline, and logo system
- Privacy/cybersecurity counsel for customer idea data, biometrics/voice, retention, and state-law review
- Franchise counsel before offering or selling any franchise
- CPA/tax advisor for entity, revenue recognition, equity interests, and multi-state implications
- Tenant representative, architect, contractor, and insurer before a site commitment
