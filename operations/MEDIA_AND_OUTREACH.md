# Media, community, and outreach plan

## Purpose

Media is a trust and learning system before it is a monetization system. Its job is to make the method legible, create qualified conversations, return useful knowledge to the community, and show honest decisions—not to turn every customer into content.

## Founding editorial promise

“We show how ideas get clearer: the question, the evidence, the tradeoff, the test, and what happened next.”

## Season 1 — eight episodes in 90 days

| Format | Count | Length | Qualified action |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Idea Clinic | 2 | 20–35 min | Submit a decision or host a clinic |
| Builder Story | 2 | 30–45 min | Share a follow-through lesson |
| Evidence Review | 2 | 12–20 min | Inspect a source register or propose a correction |
| Field Note | 2 | 8–15 min | Join a workshop or professional referral bench |

Each episode is produced for video first with a high-quality audio feed, transcript, captions, show notes, source links, disclosure, consent record, and one next action.

## Editorial rules

- Separate service agreement and media consent.
- Default to composite, hypothetical, staff-owned, or public-domain examples.
- Do not publish customer project details, names, likenesses, recordings, or outcomes without specific written permission.
- Do not make funding, legal, patentability, medical, safety, or commercial-success claims.
- Disclose sponsors and conflicts adjacent to the relevant content.
- Sponsors receive no control over conclusions or correction decisions.
- Correct material errors in the episode notes and downstream channels.
- Keep the original claim, correction date, and reason in the public corrections record.

## Distribution

- Video channel for full episodes and short explainers
- Podcast feed for audio distribution
- Email field note with one insight, one source, and one invitation
- Partner toolkits with short clips, captions, and a host-specific link
- Local live clinics recorded only when every participant has a separate path to decline

The public website begins with an email inquiry rather than a newsletter database. Add an email platform only after vendor, consent, unsubscribe, retention, and accessibility review.

## Outreach sequence

### Local proof — Months 1–6

- 20 targeted host conversations
- 3 founding host agreements
- 1 open clinic per month
- 1 professional-referral roundtable per quarter
- 1 evidence note per month

### Regional repeatability — Months 7–12

- package the best host format
- recruit 6–10 active regional hosts
- run two repeat cohorts with the same partner
- publish an outcomes report with limitations

### National learning network — Months 13–24

- invite expert correspondents and regional hosts
- test remote cohort delivery
- develop a membership only from documented continuation demand
- approach aligned sponsors only after audience quality and editorial independence are established

## Media dashboard

- episode completion and publishing cadence
- source/transcript/caption completeness
- qualified actions by episode and partner
- inquiries, booked discovery, and paid cases attributable to media
- consent exceptions and correction rate
- repeat audience and partner reuse
- production cost per qualified action

Views and downloads are secondary indicators. A smaller audience that creates qualified cases, hosts, referrals, and corrections is more valuable than broad passive reach.

## Brand language

Use: rough idea, decision, evidence, test, artifact, next move, honest uncertainty, human guided.

Avoid: genius, guaranteed, secret sauce, instant validation, AI-powered certainty, investor-ready guarantee, patentable, fundable, or “any idea for anyone.”

The working name must be tested with intended users, including people who may interpret “Lame Brain” as insulting or ableist. A rename before launch is cheaper than defending a weak brand hypothesis.
