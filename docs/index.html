07 / The first 90 days

Learn before
you build.

Three stage gates turn the central unknowns into measurable evidence.

  1. Day 0–30

    Sharpen the promise

    Interview prospective customers, test the name and four offers, define data boundaries, and secure pilot hosts.

    Gate: 30 interviews + 3 partner hosts
  2. Day 31–60

    Sell the work

    Recruit the first twelve paid concierge customers and measure acquisition, delivery time, rework, and satisfaction.

    Gate: paid conversion + positive contribution
  3. Day 61–90

    Reconcile the evidence

    Replace working assumptions with actuals, narrow the segment, revise offers, and make the next capital decision.

    Gate: repeat, revise, or stop
08 / Boundaries that protect the idea

Trust is part
of the product.

The first version is a consulting and studio service—not a law firm, patent service, broker, funding portal, or promise of capital.

01

Customer ideas

Approve retention, access, deletion, and model-training boundaries before sensitive work enters production tools.

02

Intellectual property

Document human contribution and assignment terms; refer legal clearance and filing work to qualified specialists.

03

Funding pathways

Use established rewards services and registered securities intermediaries. Keep service fees separate from transaction compensation.

04

Expansion

No franchise offering until a replicable unit, training system, quality standard, and specialist legal structure exist.
