03 / How the work runs

Make the
uncertainty visible.

We separate what you know, what you believe, and what still needs proof.

  1. 01

    Frame

    Define the decision, boundaries, people affected, and success criteria.

  2. 02

    Research

    Gather current sources and label facts, estimates, assumptions, and unknowns.

  3. 03

    Build

    Create the smallest artifact that can move the decision forward.

  4. 04

    Test

    Put the idea in front of real people, measure behavior, and record the result.

  5. 05

    Decide

    Proceed, revise, pause, refer, or stop—with a reason you can defend.
