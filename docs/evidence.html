Policy boundaries

  • No crowdfunding platform fees, investor memberships, transaction compensation, or equity interests in the launch model.
  • No permanent storefront commitment before repeatable paid demand, acceptable contribution, quality controls, and broker-backed local economics.
  • No public use of customer projects without separate, specific media consent.
  • No claim that AI output is authoritative; material claims require human review and traceable sources.
