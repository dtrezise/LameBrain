Effective 7 October 2026Pilot policy

Trust before
the big reveal.

These are public operating commitments for the founding pilot. Engagement-specific terms must be agreed in writing before confidential work begins.

This site does not use accounts, behavioral advertising, analytics trackers, or a server-side contact form. The contact form drafts a message in your own email application.

Privacy and project data

Before an engagement

Share only enough information to frame the request. Do not send passwords, financial account numbers, health information, export-controlled material, classified information, or unfiled invention details through the public contact path.

During an engagement

The written scope must name the information needed, who may access it, approved tools, storage location, retention period, deletion method, and any specialist referrals. Customer material is not sold, used for advertising, or published without separate permission.

Confidentiality

An NDA may be appropriate, but it is not a substitute for data minimization, access controls, vendor review, or secure deletion. No site copy should be read as a promise that an unsolicited email is confidential.

Responsible AI use

  • A named person remains responsible for every customer deliverable.
  • Material factual claims require traceable sources and review appropriate to their risk.
  • AI-generated content is treated as a draft or analytical aid, not as legal, medical, tax, investment, safety, or engineering authority.
  • Customer content is not placed into an AI system unless that system and use are approved for the engagement.
  • Human creative contribution, project history, and third-party rights are documented when ownership matters.
  • High-risk decisions are referred to qualified professionals rather than automated.

The operating reference is the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. Model selection should follow the current task, risk, privacy, and verification needs rather than marketing labels.

Media and consent

Paid service and media participation are separate choices. Recording, use of a name or likeness, project disclosure, editing approval, publication channel, and revocation deadline require specific consent. Declining media participation does not reduce access to paid services.

Accessibility

We aim for keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, plain language, transcripts for produced audio/video, and accessible downloadable documents. If something blocks access, email hello@lamebrainideas.com and describe the barrier and preferred format.

Corrections

Factual errors should be reported with the page, claim, and supporting source. Material corrections will be dated, propagated to related public materials, and added to the evidence register. Commercial assumptions are revised when pilot evidence replaces them.

Public-site terms

Public materials are educational and planning information. They are not an offer to sell securities and are not legal, tax, medical, investment, engineering, or patent advice. Downloads may contain estimates and forward-looking management assumptions. No engagement exists until scope, fees, responsibilities, and handling terms are accepted in writing.
